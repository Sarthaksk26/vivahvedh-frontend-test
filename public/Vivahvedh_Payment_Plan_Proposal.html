1 Platform Vision

Why a tiered membership model is essential for Vivahvedh

Vivahvedh serves a community that values trust, personal touch, and verified connections. The pricing model should reflect these values — offering enough for free users to feel the platform's quality while incentivizing upgrades through meaningful feature locks.

🎯

Conversion Through Value

Free users experience the platform quality and see profiles, creating natural FOMO to upgrade when they find a match and want to connect.

🔒

Contact Info as Premium Lock

The #1 conversion driver in matrimonial platforms — users freely browse but must upgrade to view phone numbers and email after mutual acceptance.

🏆

Office Service as Top Tier

Vivahvedh's unique strength — personal meetings, counselling, and match arrangement — becomes the premium differentiator no online-only platform can offer.

📊

Data-Driven Growth

Track conversion rates between tiers. Optimize which features to lock/unlock based on what drives the most upgrades in your specific user base.

💡 Key Insight: Industry data from Shaadi.com, Jeevansathi, and regional platforms shows that contact visibility and express interest limits are the two features that drive 80%+ of premium conversions. Our plan is structured around this insight.

4 Key Feature Descriptions

Detailed explanation of premium features that drive conversions

💌

Express Interest / Match Proposals SILVER+

Users send a formal "interest" to another profile. The receiver gets an email notification and can Accept or Decline from their dashboard. Free users can only receive — they must upgrade to send. This is the primary conversion driver.

📱

Contact Information Visibility SILVER+

Phone number and email are only revealed after both users accept the match AND the requesting user has a Silver or Gold plan. Free users see "🔒 Upgrade to view contact" — creating strong purchase intent at the moment of highest interest.

📸

Full Photo Gallery Access SILVER+

Free users see only the primary profile photo. Paid members can view the complete photo gallery with carousel navigation (swipe through all photos). Photos are a critical decision factor in matchmaking.

👁️

"Who Viewed My Profile" SILVER+

Shows a list of all users who visited your profile page. Silver users see last 30 days; Gold users see full history. Creates engagement loops — users log in frequently to check who's viewing them.

⭐

Priority Listing GOLD

Gold members' profiles appear at the top of search results with a star badge, dramatically increasing their visibility. This is a high-value perk that separates Gold from Silver.

🤝

Dedicated Offline Relationship Manager GOLD

Vivahvedh's strongest differentiator. A dedicated staff member is assigned to the Gold member and personally facilitates introductions, family meetings, and counselling — the service that online-only platforms cannot replicate.

📱

Premium WhatsApp Support GOLD

Gold members get direct access to a dedicated WhatsApp support line for real-time assistance with profile optimization, match suggestions, and meeting coordination — ensuring a seamless premium experience.

✅ Our Recommendation

We strongly recommend the 3-Tier Model (Free / Silver / Gold) for the following reasons:

  • 55% higher projected revenue compared to the 2-tier model
  • Upsell path: Free → Silver → Gold creates two conversion opportunities instead of one
  • Office services monetized: Your unique personal assistance service (meetings, counselling) is properly valued at ₹5,000 instead of being an unclear "extra fee"
  • Market positioning: Three tiers signal a premium, professional platform — aligning with Vivahvedh's trusted brand
  • Future flexibility: You can adjust prices, add seasonal offers (e.g., ₹1,500 Silver during festivals), or introduce a ₹10,000/year Platinum tier later

⚡ V1 Launch Strategy: For the initial launch, the admin will manually assign plans from the Admin Panel after confirming offline payment (bank transfer, UPI, or cash at office). Online payment gateway integration (Razorpay/Cashfree) can be added in a future phase when transaction volume justifies it.

🔐 Technical Approach (No Payment Gateway Needed)

Each user in the database will have a planType field (FREE / SILVER / GOLD) and a planExpiresAt date. The admin dashboard will have a simple "Set Plan" button next to each user. When a plan expires, the system automatically downgrades the user to Free and notifies them via email to renew.
